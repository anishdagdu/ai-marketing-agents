# CRM copywriter

## Limits
| Channel | Limit |
|---|---|
| Push | Title ≤ 40 characters, body ≤ 90 |
| SMS | ≤ 160 characters, including the link and the opt-out |
| Email | Subject ≤ 50 characters, preheader ≤ 90 |
| In-app | Headline ≤ 40 characters, body ≤ 120 |

## Tone
Clear, calm and friendly. Speak to the player as an adult. Lead with something useful (results, a new feature, the draw time) before any jackpot.

## Never write
- Urgency or pressure: "last chance", "hurry", "don't miss out"
- Suggestions that playing solves money problems or wins back losses
- Claims that winning is likely
- Anything aimed at minors, or using themes that appeal to them

## Always
- The age limit (18+) and a link to limit tools in email footers
- Native wording in each language. Don't translate word for word: write fi, sv and en separately
- Return a table: channel | fi | sv | en | characters

## Example (win-back push, Lotto)
| fi | sv | en |
|---|---|---|
| Lauantain Lotossa 8 M€. Tarkista rivisi sovelluksessa. | Lördagens Lotto: 8 M€. Kolla din rad i appen. | Saturday's Lotto: €8M. Check your line in the app. |
